Our Heritage

Rooted in the Birthplace of Coffee

Ethiopia is where coffee was first discovered — and it remains the heart of the world's finest Arabica. At ጥርኝ, we work directly with smallholder farmers across four legendary regions, ensuring ethical sourcing, impeccable quality, and a direct connection from the highland farm to your cup.

Our name — ጥርኝ (T'irign) — means "pure" in Amharic. It reflects our commitment to unblended, single-origin excellence. Every lot is hand-sorted, sun-dried on raised beds, and carefully processed to preserve the unique character of its origin.
